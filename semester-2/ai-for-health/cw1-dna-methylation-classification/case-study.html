866,519 raw probes become sixteen numbers, and the eight subtypes separate on their own

Raw IDAT files from two incompatible Illumina methylation arrays — the older HM450 and the newer EPIC — were each corrected, harmonised into one shared 22,893-probe feature space, and factorised into 16 NMF metagenes. Before those metagenes ever reach a classifier, a UMAP projection already shows what the pipeline was worth: all eight consensus medulloblastoma subtypes fall into distinct, biologically coherent clusters.

01 · UMAP finishes what PCA starts

The first three linear PCA components explain only 45% of variance and blur adjacent subtypes. Fitting UMAP on the first 20 PCs instead cleanly separates all eight consensus subtypes, with WNT/SHH forming one supercluster and the two Group 4 subtypes standing furthest apart — matching the original subtype literature.

02 · MissForest lost to the clock, not the model

MissForest failed to converge after 90+ minutes on a 97×22,893 matrix, so imputation defaulted to KNN and MICE (IterativeImputer) for the 14,438 probes under 30% missing. The 43 probes missing in 100% of EPIC samples were substituted directly from HM450 — justified by a 0.95 same-patient beta correlation across platforms.

03 · Sixteen metagenes, chosen by consensus not convenience

Cophenetic correlation across k=14–20 NMF components selected k=16 as the most stable clustering point. The resulting 1,370×16 metagene matrix — a single 0.3MB CSV, down from an original 12GB, 866,519-probe dataset — is Part 2's entire training input.

Methods

Correct each array, share one feature space, then let NMF summarise it

Every EPIC sample was corrected with pylluminator's SeSAMe-style pipeline — type-I channel inference, p-OOBAH detection masking, NOOB background correction, non-linear dye-bias correction — before HM450 and EPIC were reduced to a shared, SD-ranked set of 25,000 probes and matched down to 22,893 features present, at acceptable missingness, on both platforms. NMF was chosen as the actual feature-producing step over PCA or UMAP because its non-negativity constraint keeps components biologically interpretable as methylation “metagenes”, whereas PCA and UMAP were kept as validation-only visualisations to check the feature space was worth factorising at all.

actual pipeline order executed in the notebook · PCA and UMAP (not shown) run in parallel on the 22,893-probe matrix purely to validate separability before NMF

The clearest caveat carries straight into Part 2, by the notebook's own admission: NMF was fit on the full 1,370-sample HM450 set before any train/test split existed, so the 16 metagenes feeding Part 2's classifier aren't strictly independent of its eventual test data — refitting NMF on training folds only is flagged as follow-up work. MissForest was abandoned for cost, not accuracy, so how it would have compared to KNN/MICE remains unverified. TSNE was imported alongside PCA, UMAP and NMF at the top of the notebook but never actually run — UMAP alone did the non-linear separability check. And the HM450 manifest's own quality-mask flagged nothing beyond sex-chromosome probes in this run, so all further probe attrition here is missingness-driven rather than manifest-driven.

Data

1,370 HM450 + 97 EPIC samples, aligned to one 22,893-probe space

All samples come from a single GEO series, GSE130051, labelled with Sharma et al.'s (2019) eight consensus molecular subtypes of Group 3 and Group 4 medulloblastoma — the same imbalanced class split (WNT at 51 samples, Group4-B at 347) that Part 2 classifies. Of 1,501 samples downloaded, those with no assigned subtype (MBNOS) were dropped, and 2 EPIC samples too sparse to impute responsibly (>30% of probes missing) were dropped too.
