One dopamine receptor turns an approved drug into four repurposing candidates

SPARQL metapath queries over the OREGANO graph (Compound→Protein→Gene→Disease) surfaced Pardoprunox — a drug with no listed indications in the graph — as a candidate treatment for four conditions it isn't linked to, all via a shared dopamine-receptor pathway. A companion Bayesian network then ranked repurposing candidates for five separate diseases from graph topology alone, correctly recovering an FDA-approved hypertension drug and an IL-6 inhibitor still in diabetes trials.

the Pardoprunox metapath: one compound, three dopamine-receptor targets, four disease hypotheses

01 · The longer metapath won on mechanism

The direct Compound→Gene→Disease path has more links, but Compound→Protein→Gene→Disease was chosen because it matches pharmacology's "mechanism of action" model — drugs act on proteins, not genes directly — and fits a graph built around compound–target relationships.

02 · One compound, one receptor family, four diseases

Pardoprunox targets three dopamine-receptor proteins that, via three genes, connect to Dystonia, Tremor, Schizophrenia and ADHD — none listed as an indication in the graph. NCBI confirms the gene–disease links, but the graph can't say whether Pardoprunox would help or worsen any of them.

03 · The network got three of five right

Ranking by an F2-weighted target-overlap prior and MCMC sampling, the network's top pick correctly recovered an FDA-approved hypertension drug and flagged an experimental diabetes therapy — but for two other diseases it surfaced compounds that would worsen the condition or aren't real drugs at all.

Methods

A causal DAG built by inverting one ontology edge and pruning another

The ontology encodes what's valid, not what's uncertain — so treatment likelihood needed a genuinely causal structure, not just a semantic one. The network models the joint distribution P(G,C,P,D) — Gene and Compound as root causes, Protein as a mediator conditioned on both, Disease as the outcome conditioned on Protein — validated against the Imatinib/Bcr-Abl mechanism in chronic myeloid leukaemia, where causality really does flow Compound→Protein←Gene. Two deliberate departures from the ontology made this possible: the gene_product_of edge (Protein→Gene) was reversed to Gene→Protein to reflect that genes cause proteins, not the other way round, and the direct Compound→Disease edge was pruned entirely, forcing every inference through the Protein node.

P(G,C,P,D) = P(D|P)·P(P|G,C)·P(C)·P(G) · dashed edge = the ontology relation this structure reverses

Inference was framed as diagnostic reasoning: observing the healthy state (D = False), which compound C maximises P(C|D=False)? Because OREGANO has no binding-affinity data, a uniform prior over compounds was replaced with an F2-weighted prior based on target overlap with the disease pathway — chosen over a plain specificity score because specificity unfairly punished "dirty drugs" with several genuinely valid targets. Compounds were then ranked by frequency in a Categorical Gibbs-Metropolis MCMC trace. The model has three limitations stated plainly in the notebook: it treats compound choice as a single categorical variable, so it cannot model polypharmacy; it has no notion of agonist vs. antagonist, so it is equally likely to predict a cure and a poison; and it discretises protein activity to binary normal/abnormal states, which cannot capture partial inhibition. The F2 choice also backfired once — for Leber Optic Atrophy it pushed non-drug metabolites into the top three purely because they hit multiple pathway proteins.

Data

Five diseases, one shared graph, wildly different pathway sizes

Schizophrenia, Diabetes, Hypertension, CFC Syndrome and Leber Optic Atrophy (LHON) were chosen to span common, complex and rare genetic conditions. For each, SPARQL extracted the subgraph of genes, proteins and compounds connected to that disease — discarding compounds with effectively zero probability of a link, both for computational efficiency and because a zero-pathway compound has no basis for the F2 prior at all.
